Godot(C#) - Dundee Hooley - Programmer - 4 Weeks

Tide Together

Tide Together is a relaxing 2-player co-op experience exhibited at the Dundee Hooley festival, an annual St Andrews Day festival. The Hooley is an all-ages family event, so our game was developed to be playable with only the analogue stick of a Nintendo Switch Joy-Con, lowering the barrier of entry for parents and young children. Our scope was intentionally kept small to ensure extra time for polish and bug fixing.

My Responsibilities:

  • Player Movement
  • Shaders & Post Processing
  • Moving background stars (MultiMesh)
